    The method

    Don't chase trends. Build franchises.

    Proven formats became series with new twists, places and prizes: last hand on the box, trivia with stakes, a stranger picks your outfit, handcuffed to a stranger, iPad or crush confession.

    01

    Mine the catalogFind the videos that already over-performed.

    02

    Isolate the variableWork out which element moved the views: the hook, the stakes, the twist.

    03

    Build the franchiseTurn it into a repeatable format, then add growth mechanics.
